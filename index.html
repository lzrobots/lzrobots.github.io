About me

I am a full professor at the School of Data Science, Fudan University, where I lead the Logos Robotics Group. I also hold a joint appointment at the Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII).

I completed my PhD in computer science at Queen Mary University of London under professor Tao Xiang, and was subsequently a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Engineering Science at the University of Oxford with professor Philip H.S. Torr and professor Andrew Zisserman, followed by a role as Research Scientist at Samsung AI Center Cambridge.

🤖 Logos Robotics Group 🤖

The goal of our group is to build intelligence for robotics, which falls under physical AI.

Specific areas of interest include embodied intelligence 🦾, general-purpose multimodal large models 🧠 and agents 🧑‍💻, and—to a lesser extent—3D vision and autonomous driving 🚗.

Interested in joining the group?

If you are highly creative, have top grades/coding skill and interested in joining my group please do not hesitate to send me your CV and transcripts of grades.
Make sure you are super enthusiastic about AI research.

Highlighted papers (All papers)

X-WBC: A Cross-Embodiment Foundation Model for Humanoid Whole-Body Control

Juntong Zhang, Chun Gu, Li Zhang

CoRL 2026

[paper][project page][code]

This paper introduces a shared model for whole-body control across humanoids with different bodies. A common Transformer captures reusable motion structure, while robot-specific modules translate human motion and sparse VR commands into actions suited to each robot.

SMR: Learning Streaming Motion Retargeting for Humanoids from Human Semantic

Chun Gu, Juntong Zhang, Jingyu Li, Li Zhang

arXiv preprint, 2026

[paper][project page][code]

This paper studies how humanoids can preserve the intent of human motion while respecting their own physical constraints. Physics-based experts provide trajectories for training a causal model that generates robot motion references from streaming human observations.

Training in Deployment: Human-in-the-loop Online Post-Training for Robot Manipulation

Chun Gu, Xiaofei Wei, Yiming Chen, Bozhou Zhang, Juntong Zhang, Xiatian Zhu, Li Zhang

arXiv preprint, 2026

[paper][project page][code]

This paper presents a framework that combines autonomous execution, human corrections, and online policy updates during robot deployment. TIDE distinguishes human demonstrations from the robot’s own experience, allowing both to improve the policy through supervised learning.

ROVE: Unlocking Human Interventions for Humanoid Manipulation via Reinforcement Learning

Wei Xiao, Weiliang Tang, Yuying Ge, Hui Zhou, Yao Mu, Li Zhang, Yixiao Ge

arXiv preprint, 2026

[paper][project page]

This paper studies how humanoids can learn from human corrections that are not always expert demonstrations. Reinforcement learning identifies valuable behaviors in mixed-quality experience, while human videos help the value model recognize failures and recoveries.

UltraTex: Unleashing 2K Multi-View Diffusion for 3D Texturing

Yibo Zhang, Ze Yuan, Nan Cao, Li Zhang, Yanpei Cao, Yuanchen Guo, Rui Ma

SIGGRAPH Asia 2026

[paper][project page][github][huggingface]

This paper introduces an efficient framework for generating detailed 2K textures on 3D assets with multi-view diffusion. It removes background tokens and sparsifies foreground attention, while foreground-aware decoding preserves the fine detail of the generated textures.

Forward Dynamics Models

Zipei Ma, Junzhe Jiang, Jiahui Zhang, Juntong Zhang, Chun Gu, Bozhou Zhang, Jiankang Deng, Xiatian Zhu, Li Zhang

arXiv preprint, 2026

[paper][project page][code]

This paper uses predictions of an action’s consequences to improve robot policies during training. An asymmetric architecture lets future-state supervision shape action features while keeping future generation out of the deployment-time control loop.

TacPAC: Tactile Prediction and Real-Time Action Correction in World-Action Models for Contact-Rich Manipulation

Zipei Ma, Xiaofei Wei, Junzhe Jiang, Shunlin Lu, Li Zhang

arXiv preprint, 2026

[paper][project page][code]

This paper introduces tactile prediction and real-time action correction for contact-rich manipulation. TacPAC compares predicted touch with live tactile feedback and uses the differences to correct upcoming actions without interrupting execution.

Learning Transferable Dynamics Priors from Action to World Modeling

Ze Huang, Jiahui Zhang, Hairuo Liu, Chenxi Zhang, Ran Cheng, Li Zhang

ECCV 2026

[paper][project page][code]

This paper learns a transferable dynamics prior by pretraining a world model on diverse robot manipulation data. The same prior supports both long-horizon visual simulation and an instruction-conditioned policy that jointly predicts future observations and robot actions.

From Imagined Futures to Executable Actions: Mixture of Latent Actions for Robot Manipulation

Yajie Li, Bozhou Zhang, Chun Gu, Zipei Ma, Jiahui Zhang, Jiankang Deng, Xiatian Zhu, Li Zhang

ICML 2026

[paper][project page][code]

This paper bridges the gap between visually plausible imagined futures and executable robot actions. A mixture of inverse dynamics models extracts complementary motion, depth, and semantic cues from predicted visual transitions to guide robot control.

NavOL: Navigation Policy with Online Imitation Learning

Xiaofei Wei, Chun Gu, Li Zhang

ICML 2026

[paper][project page][code]

This paper trains navigation policies on the states they visit, using a global planner to supply expert guidance in simulation. This online imitation loop reduces compounding errors and transfers to real-world navigation without additional training.

Disentangled Robot Learning via Separate Forward and Inverse Dynamics Pretraining

Wenyao Zhang, Bozhou Zhang, Zekun Qi, Wenjun Zeng, Xin Jin, Li Zhang

ICLR 2026

[paper][code]

This paper separates future prediction from action inference during pretraining so robots can learn from human and robot videos without action labels. The two models are then combined and jointly fine-tuned for downstream manipulation tasks.

DriveWeaver: Point-Conditioned Video Inpainting for Controllable Vehicle Insertion in Autonomous Driving Simulation

Junzhe Jiang, Zipei Ma, Zijie Pan, Li Zhang

ECCV 2026

[paper][code]

This paper introduces controllable vehicle insertion into driving scenes through video inpainting guided by point clouds. A hierarchical generation strategy maintains each vehicle’s appearance over time, while reconstruction into 3D Gaussians makes the results usable in real-time simulation.

Periodic Vibration Gaussian: Dynamic Urban Scene Reconstruction and Real-time Rendering

Yurui Chen, Chun Gu, Junzhe Jiang, Xiatian Zhu, Li Zhang

IJCV 2026

[paper][project page][code]

This paper models static structures and moving objects together by extending 3D Gaussian splatting with time-varying, periodic motion. The unified representation reconstructs dynamic urban scenes from sparse observations and supports real-time rendering without manually labeling individual vehicles.

Reinforcing Action Policies by Prophesying

Jiahui Zhang, Ze Huang, Chun Gu, Zipei Ma, Li Zhang

arXiv preprint, 2025

[paper][project page][code]

This paper uses a learned action-to-video world model as a simulator for improving robot policies beyond imitation. ProphRL combines this model with reinforcement learning tailored to flow-based action generation, enabling policies to learn more reliable strategies before deployment on real robots.

4D-VLA: Spatiotemporal Vision-Language-Action Pretraining with Cross-Scene Calibration

Jiahui Zhang, Yurui Chen, Yueming Xu, Ze Huang, Yanpeng Zhou, Yu-Jie Yuan, Xinyue Cai, Guowei Huang, Xingyue Quan, Hang Xu, Li Zhang

NeurIPS 2025

[paper][code]

This paper uses depth and visual history to give robot policies a more consistent understanding of where objects are and how scenes evolve. Coordinate alignment and informative frame selection improve learning across diverse demonstrations.
